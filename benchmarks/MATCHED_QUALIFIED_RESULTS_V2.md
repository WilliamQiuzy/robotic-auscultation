# Closed comparison reporting contract

`matched-qualified-results-v2.schema.json` describes the November 6 closed
comparison. Its canonical artifact is `docs/evidence/rebuilt-matched-20261106-v3.json`.
It retains all eight frozen scenarios and all three assisted systems. Each
physical verdict binds the original per-case native qualification, terminal
owner, native execution/audit and source-plan match. ACT and Astra additionally
bind actual model invocation. Software exit, a video or a missing audit cannot
supply a positive verdict.

The original native qualifier is authoritative. Some small reporting summaries
do not contain measured hold-force extrema or local-plane extrema; those fields
remain null. This does not mean raw force was never measured: the original
qualifier consumed actual 240 Hz records and checked the unchanged continuous
3 s, 3.5–4.5 N hold gate. Raw traces remain private on Linux. Acceptance limits
must never be inserted as measured extrema. This is
a reporting contract, not a change to any physical gate or an acceptance claim
for the full project, hardware, acoustics or clinical use.

The original `matched-eight-results-v1` schema/template and partial/pending
receipts remain immutable historical preparation artifacts. V2 records the
closed snapshot: 24 actual native episodes, 16 qualified completions (expert
8/8, ACT 0/8, Astra plus analytic expert 8/8), with zero unknown verdicts. The
Astra/expert tie does not establish improved motor control or causal sensor use.
Astra latency, token usage and unknown pricing/HTTP-only latency retain their
separate meanings in the canonical summary.
