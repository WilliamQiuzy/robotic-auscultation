01 / The learning loop

Teach the approach.
Measure the contact.

The current baseline learns collision checked approach trajectories. A local controller handles force, dwell time and release. Every completed episode must return home.

  1. 01

    Build the scene

    OpenArm, spring loaded stethoscope, seated body and the approved RGB + depth view.

  2. 02

    Query the expert

    Plan from the current state; retain actual commands, measured motion and contact.

  3. 03

    Export valid windows

    Filter by phase and ownership. Split by body identity before fitting normalization.

  4. 04

    Train official ACT

    Predict 20 actions. Execute the first 10, observe again, then replan.

  5. 05

    Run closed loop

    Score the actual approach, contact, hold, withdrawal and return, beyond action error.

State-only ACT control contract

A small, explicit control baseline.

Official LeRobot state-only ACT. q7 + target-relative position3 + rotation6D + confidence1 = state17; H2 gives 34 features. H20 targets use one fixed joint origin per block.

state17 × 2H20 / E10FP32TRAIN only normalization

The published ACT uses state and target geometry. RGB + depth and Hall are recorded; Hall drives the analytic contact controller. This ACT is not a multimodal VLA, and simulated audio is not used.

04 / Research questions

What does this
preview establish?

The scope matters as much as the score. These answers describe the released evidence and the work that remains.

Is this a clinical or physical robot result?

This page reports Isaac Sim experiments. It does not establish clinical performance or a completed physical robot validation.

Do the new 8/8, 0/8 and 8/8 use the same scenarios?

Yes:2 held-out identities ×2 known seated poses × R3/L3,8 independent home-start episodes per system. The systems have different learned/analytic motor roles; this is not a model ranking. Historical 8/8 and 0/2 below are separate older cohorts.

What does the ACT policy observe?

Two state17 frames give 34 features: q7, given-target-relative position3, rotation6D and confidence1. ACT has no RGB, depth or Hall model input; analytic contact uses Hall. Given 3D targets are not trained visual localization.

Why can lower action error still fail?

The 20,000-update fit selected step 4,500 on full selection-VAL target error. In the frozen comparison, six ACT cases stopped at posture gates and two at mesh-clearance gates. A TRAIN/selection coverage diagnosis suggests further pose/recovery experiments, not a proven causal fix.

Has Astra executed a simulator episode?

Yes. The original native qualifier accepted all 8 Astra-supervised episodes, with 24 actual gpt-6-astra calls and analytic local motor execution. The expert also passed 8/8; this does not show Astra improved the controller or used any modality causally.

What can I reproduce from this release?

Pinned source, CPU checks, contracts, scalar evidence and three actual matched recordings are public. Raw arrays, weights and licensed body assets are not bundled; actual simulator reproduction needs its separately provisioned Linux/NVIDIA runtime and fresh local qualification.
